PC TeleRC v0.1.1rc7 — PWM noise capture and recovery fixes (test candidate)

Converter 1.0.5 fixes a reproduced capture defect: a short low notch within a valid forward PWM pulse could be interpreted as reverse or neutral, or invalidate the pulse and eventually trigger the signal-loss latch. Falling edges now require 30 us of stable low, preserving the original rise across shorter notches. Original pulse age, 150 ms loss stop and 300 ms neutral recovery remain.

USB CDC diagnostic waits are bounded to 1 ms in converter 1.0.5, Wi-Fi/ELRS bridge 0.1.5 and SX1262 pair 0.2.1. Separate steering/throttle sensitivity remains in the Windows app.

Build outputs: Windows installer and portable, full source, role-specific firmware sketches and compiled target images, checksums. Flash the converter for the PWM correction; update only the other board roles installed on your rover. Read FIRMWARE-RECOVERY-rc7.md before flashing. No FC parameter changes are applied automatically.

Validation includes host fault injection on actual sketch sources and five ESP32-S3 target builds. Hardware ground-stop causality, voltage/current behavior and radio range remain unverified. This candidate does not replace v0.1.0a10 as the stable baseline.
